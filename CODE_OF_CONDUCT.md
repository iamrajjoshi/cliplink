# Code of conduct

People with different backgrounds and levels of experience are welcome in Cliplink's issues, pull requests, and other project spaces. Help keep those conversations useful:

- Discuss the work without personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.
- Respect people's privacy. Ask before sharing someone else's personal information or private messages.
- Give specific feedback and leave room for questions or mistakes.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project; avoid spam and repeated unwanted contact.

## Reporting a problem

Send conduct concerns privately to Raj Joshi at [rajjoshi.0222@gmail.com](mailto:rajjoshi.0222@gmail.com). Include links or context that help explain what happened, and leave out unrelated personal information. Reports will only be shared as needed to assess or address the concern.

Raj may remove content, close a conversation, or restrict participation in response to violations. Affected participants can use the same address to ask about a decision or provide more context.

For software vulnerabilities, use [Security](SECURITY.md).
